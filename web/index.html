DSU Remote: your phone as a Wii Remote

        
DSU Remote requires the local Python launcher (serve.py); GitHub Pages cannot connect to the phone's UDP service directly. On your local network, type the phone IP and port 26760.

      

      

      

      

      

        
Wii Remote
Cross = A · Square = B · Triangle = 1 · Circle = 2
Turning the phone moves the pointer, shaking it shakes the Wii Remote, touching the screen centers the pointer.

        
Nunchuk (a second phone)
Shaking it shakes the Nunchuk · stick = Nunchuk stick
Cross/ZL/ZR = Z · other buttons = C

        
Classic controller ("Classic" layout, one phone)
Left stick = Nunchuk · right stick = pointer
a = A · b/ZR = B · ZL = Z · L = C · R = shake Nunchuk

        
Keyboard, mouse and controller keep working at the same time. With a single phone in Wii Remote mode, use the keyboard or a controller for the Nunchuk (WASD, R = shake).

      

    

    

      
Display

      
Full screenAlso with the button that appears at the top right when you move the mouse.

      
Show FPSSmall counter at the top left (frames per second, and whether shaders are being compiled).

      
Show info barThe bar at the bottom with the status and the log (in case something goes wrong).

      
Internal resolution2x looks sharper; 1x is much lighter on the graphics card (useful on laptops). Applied when you press Play.

        

      
Shaders

      
Shader modePrecompile everything: all known shaders are prepared on the loading screen; a new one is drawn with the generic shader while it compiles (like Dolphin's "hybrid" mode).
        Generic shader only: never compiles during the game (no shader stutter) but is heavier on the graphics card.

        

      
Shaders seen in your gamesThey are saved automatically and precompiled next time. You can export them to have them added to the port.
